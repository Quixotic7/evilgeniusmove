# EvilGenius for Move: QA pass, 16 November 2026

Build tested: commit `e644745` plus a longer test state line (uncommitted).

## Verdict

You can build a two-pattern song with it today, but not the way a Move player would expect to. The core loop is
solid: steps, held-step editing on eight knobs, playing and recording notes from the pads, the sound editor, effects,
the mix, patterns, saving. Audio on the unit is clean.

Three things stop it being usable without knowing the workarounds:

1. **You cannot choose a sound.** Shift + wheel opens the preset browser and then starts changing the track instead.
   While the browser is open the step buttons silently stop making steps.
2. **A quick Shift press sticks.** The core treats a short Shift tap as "Shift stays on". That breaks Shift + wheel,
   Shift + Sampling and Shift + −/+, and leaves the screen on the core's Shift page.
3. **The step buttons change page under your fingers while it plays.** The pattern is two pages long and the view
   follows the playhead, so "step 5" is a different step every two bars.

Fix those three and it becomes a usable instrument for someone coming from Move.

## How it was tested

I played the part of a Move owner building a track from the title screen: a beat, a bass line, a sound, effects, a
mix, a second pattern, an arrangement, a save. Nine scripted sessions (`tests/qa/`) ran in the simulator, with the
screen captured at each step and the instrument's state read back (steps per track, pattern, tempo, mutes, what the
core has open). Two more sessions ran on the Move itself over SSH through the test hook.

What this could not cover:

- **Sound quality and feel.** I can measure that sound comes out and how loud, not whether it sounds right or whether
  the pads feel good.
- **LED colours.** I can see which LED packets are sent, not what they look like.
- **The power button.** It needs a finger.
- **Anything a person does by accident**, beyond the cases I thought to try.

## What works

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Steps | Tap makes and removes a step. Hold shows `STEP n` and its notes. |
| Held-step knobs | Velocity, length, micro, ratchet, chance, condition, trig and slide all change the held step; the pop-up shows the value. With nothing held they set the track's defaults. |
| Notes into a step | Hold a step, press a pad: the note goes in. |
| Recording | Record while stopped waits, then captures from your first note (4 of 4 notes landed). Record while playing overdubs. |
| Undo / redo | Both work on step edits. |
| Tracks | Top-row pads and track buttons select. Mute + track mutes; Shift + Mute + track solos. |
| Sound editor | 17 pages, eight knobs each, group pads, wheel paging, pop-ups, all-eight view. A knob turned with a step held locks that step. |
| Effects | Nine floors with their own pictures, the lift ride, sends. Echo and Room use all eight knobs. |
| Mix | Six faders on six knobs, live meters. |
| Patterns | Loop + step selects or queues; the pop-up shows `A02>A01` while queued. Copy and Shift + Copy copy and paste a pattern. Banks work. |
| Tempo, metronome, setup menu, projects page | All open and work with Shift held properly. |
| Saving | Edits survive a quit one third of a second after the last change, in the simulator and on the unit. |
| Audio on the unit | 0 late audio blocks in 30 s of the busiest demo pattern across every page. Audio thread about 5% of one core, UI thread about 2%, 14 MB of memory. |

## Blockers

### 1. Choosing a sound is broken

- **What happens:** with Shift held, the first wheel click opens the core's preset browser. Every click after that
  changes the selected *track*, because once the browser is open the wheel is passed to the core with Shift still
  down, and the core reads Shift + SELECT as "track".
- **With a quick Shift press:** the first click changes the track's root note instead (C to C#), then the browser
  opens and the track's sound changes to another track's.
- **Then you are trapped:** the browser stays open with no hint how to leave. Step buttons pick presets instead of
  making steps. In one session I tapped four steps, played, and heard nothing: no steps had been made.
- **Knock-on:** an EvilGenius drum kit is one track with a different sound locked to each step. That lock is also
  Shift + wheel, so a kit on one track cannot be built. The workaround is one drum sound per track.
- **Recommendation:** give sound choice its own Move page. The wheel scrolls presets, the name shows full screen,
  the pads audition, wheel click accepts, Back cancels. With a step held, the same page sets that step's sound.

### 2. Shift sticks after a short press

- **Cause:** the core latches Shift when its button is released quickly (`shift_stick` in `ctl.c`). The Move layer
  also releases and re-presses the core's Shift on purpose around some actions, which the core reads as that tap.
- **Seen as:**
  - Shift + wheel changing the root note.
  - Shift + Sampling opening Projects rather than Save Preset, where a wheel click then saved the whole project.
  - After Shift + −, the screen stayed on the core's Shift page through an entire recording.
- **Recommendation:** stop sending the core's Shift button at all. Keep Shift as a Move-layer modifier and call the
  core functions each shortcut needs directly. Until then, clear the core's sticky flag every time.

### 3. Step buttons follow the playing page

- **What happens:** the demo patterns are 32 steps, two pages. While it plays, the page shown flips every two bars.
  I held "step 5" to edit my kick and got `STEP 5 EMPTY`: page two. Tapping a step to remove it added one on the
  other page instead.
- **No warning:** the `PAGE n` pop-up appears only when you press Left or Right, not when the page follows.
- **Recommendation:** turn page follow off by default on Move, so the buttons stay on the page you chose. Show which
  page is playing another way, for example a pulse on Left or Right.

## Minor issues

- **Two Plays to start.** The first Play only leaves the title. Pads at the title do nothing.
- **`PATTERN` for both copy and paste.** The core's second line (COPIED, PASTED) is not shown, so the pop-up reads the
  same for both.
- **Wheel at home changes step page**, the same as Left and Right. It could do something of its own.
- **Platforms are thin and all alike on a drum track.** Four kicks draw as four short dashes at one height.
- **The agent stands on top of step 1's platform** when stopped.
- **Mix pages two and three** (sends, FX mix) are cropped core drawings and read as stripes.
- **Name pop-ups cover what you just opened** for 0.7 s, including the browser and projects lists.
- **The SETTINGS item** holds the core's HOME button for half a second to open its settings. Nothing breaks, but it
  is slow and depends on a timing constant.
- **Knob 1 in the core's settings** changes the home view. I changed mine to Geek Out by accident while testing.

## What a Move player will look for and not find

| Move habit | Here |
|---|---|
| Shift + step 9, keys and scales | Not mapped |
| Shift + step 7, groove | Not mapped; swing is not reachable |
| Shift + step 10, full velocity | Not mapped |
| Shift + step 15, quantize | Not mapped; the core has it in TOOLS |
| Note repeat | Not mapped; the core has ratchets per step |
| 16 drum pads, one drum each | Pads are always a scale layout of one sound |
| Capture | Opens FX. The core's own capture is on Record while stopped, which is close but unlabelled |
| Session view | No equivalent; patterns are chosen with Loop + step |
| Per-track clip lengths | Not reachable |

## Recommendations, in order

1. **Sound browser as a Move page**, including the per-step sound lock. This unblocks drum kits.
2. **Take the core's Shift out of the path.** One change removes a whole family of misfires.
3. **Page follow off by default**, with a visible cue for the playing page.
4. **Fix the all-eight view's spacing.**
5. **Back, −/+ and Menu at home:** octave on −/+, nothing on Back, room only on Menu.
6. **Scale and root page** on Shift + step 9, and **length** on Loop + wheel.
7. **Copy and Delete on steps.**
8. **A blank project** from the setup menu.
9. **Then the remaining core pages**, by how often they are used.

## Follow-up, the same day: the three blockers

Fixed and tried again in the simulator and on the unit (`tests/qa/10-fixes.txt`).

| Blocker | Now |
|---|---|
| 1. Choosing a sound | Shift + wheel opens a sound page drawn for this screen (`SOUND 8/95`, the name large). The wheel steps through the sounds, each heard on the track as it comes up; the pads audition; click takes it; Back keeps your own. Step buttons do nothing while it is open. With a step held, Shift + wheel sets that step's own sound and shows its name, so a kit on one track can be built. |
| 2. Shift sticking | The core never receives a Shift any more. Each Shift shortcut calls the core's function by name. A quick Shift + wheel no longer changes the root, Shift + Sampling opens Save Preset, and the screen stays on the room after Shift + −. |
| 3. Steps following the playhead | Page follow is off at start. The page shown stays through both pages of playback, and a held step 5 is step 5. Left or Right lights on the side the playhead is on. |

Also changed, because the fixes touched them:

- **Back** now closes whatever page is open (it sends the core's HOME), steps back a level in a settings menu, and does
  nothing at home. This fixes Back changing the entry octave (major issue 5, in part) and Back not leaving Save Preset.
- **Shift + step 11** opens the MIDI FX page rather than toggling bypass (major issue 10).

Found while retesting:

- **Record while stopped replaces the track.** Arming while stopped is the core's CAPTURE: nothing changes until the
  first note, then the track is cleared and recorded afresh. A four-note bass line became one note. Undo brings it
  back, but a Move player will not expect it. Recommendation: make overdub the default, and keep capture for an
  empty track.
- **Page follow is forced off at every start**, so someone who wants it on has to switch it on again each time.

Still open from the list above: 4, 5 (−/+ alone), 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and the minor issues.
